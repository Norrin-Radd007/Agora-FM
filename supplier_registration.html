Supplier Terms & Conditions

Last updated: April 2026 · Version 3.0 · Agora FM Ltd, Registered in England & Wales

1. Definitions

"Agora FM" means Agora FM Ltd, the operator of the Agora FM platform. "Supplier" means any company or individual registered on the platform to provide FM services. "Customer" means any organisation using the platform to commission FM services. "Platform" means the Agora FM web-based marketplace at agorafm.co.uk and associated applications.

2. Registration & Verification

2.1 All information provided at registration must be accurate, complete and kept up to date. 2.2 Agora FM reserves the right to verify all accreditations, insurance documents and company information prior to activating a supplier profile. 2.3 Profiles found to contain false or misleading information will be suspended immediately and may be permanently removed. 2.4 Suppliers must notify Agora FM within 7 days of any change to their accreditation status or insurance cover.

3. Commission Structure

3.1 Agora FM charges a commission of 5% of the gross contract value on all jobs completed through the platform. 3.2 Commission is deducted at the point of payment settlement and is not charged to the customer. 3.3 The commission rate may be reviewed annually with 90 days' written notice to suppliers. 3.4 No commission is payable on jobs that are cancelled prior to commencement, provided the supplier notifies Agora FM within 48 hours of cancellation.

4. Payment Terms

4.1 Suppliers select their preferred payment terms (30, 60 or 90 days) at registration. 4.2 Payment is processed by Agora FM upon receipt of a confirmed job completion report from both parties. 4.3 Agora FM reserves the right to withhold payment pending resolution of any customer dispute. 4.4 Payments are made via BACS to the bank account details provided at registration. Suppliers are responsible for ensuring bank details are accurate and current.

5. Service Standards

5.1 Suppliers must maintain all accreditations and certifications listed on their profile at all times. 5.2 All work must be carried out by suitably qualified and insured engineers. 5.3 Suppliers must respond to customer enquiries within 2 business days. 5.4 Emergency callout requests must receive an acknowledgement within 4 hours. 5.5 Suppliers must provide all required compliance documentation (service reports, certificates) within 5 business days of job completion.

6. Insurance Requirements

6.1 Suppliers must maintain public liability insurance of a minimum of £2 million per occurrence and employers liability insurance of a minimum of £5 million. 6.2 Proof of insurance must be provided at registration and upon each annual renewal. 6.3 Agora FM must be notified immediately if insurance lapses or is reduced below the required minimum levels.

7. Intellectual Property & Data

7.1 Suppliers grant Agora FM a non-exclusive licence to display their company name, logo, description, accreditations and pricing on the platform. 7.2 Agora FM will process supplier personal data in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data protection legislation including UK GDPR. 7.3 Suppliers must not use customer data obtained through the platform for any purpose other than fulfilling the commissioned service.

8. Reviews & Ratings

8.1 Customers may submit verified reviews following job completion. Agora FM will not remove negative reviews unless they are found to be fraudulent or in breach of platform guidelines. 8.2 Suppliers may respond to reviews via their supplier dashboard. 8.3 Agora FM reserves the right to suspend suppliers whose average rating falls below 3.0 stars following a minimum of 10 reviews.

9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' written notice. 9.2 Agora FM may suspend or terminate a supplier account immediately in cases of fraud, misrepresentation, serious breach of service standards, or reputational risk to the platform. 9.3 Upon termination, any outstanding payments owed to the supplier will be settled within 30 days, subject to deduction of any outstanding commission or disputed amounts.

10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 Agora FM acts solely as an intermediary marketplace and is not a party to any contract between supplier and customer. 10.2 Agora FM's total liability to any supplier in any 12-month period shall not exceed the total commission paid by that supplier in the same period. 10.3 Agora FM shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential or economic loss arising from use of the platform.

11. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
